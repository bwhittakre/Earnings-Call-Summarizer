## CS-2 — THE FULL ROZ PROCESS

**Header:** WHAT ACTUALLY RUNS ON A NEW NAME

**Body (seven steps, one line each):**

1. **Pull** — Quartr MCP only. Every FY earnings call and every
   conference / investor-day transcript, Q&A tails included.
2. **Score** — Structured Narrative, eight dimensions, four stages:
   level (this quarter), delta (vs last call), surprise (vs Street),
   novelty (new objects on the three narrative dims).
3. **Join** — feature panel + `quant_z_pit`. The print versus this
   company’s own surprise history, as of the call date.
4. **Brief** — Post-Call Brief. The human page. Five questions.
5. **Remember** — Claims Desk trees. Verbatim clocks, not adjectives.
6. **This meeting** — call scorecard. Cited, restated, silent.
   Delivery among aged trees only.
7. **Rank (parked)** — narrative–quant agreement ranked names on XLK.
   Not live on these two.

**Footer:** Conferences seed clocks. FY restates or ignores them. **A FY-only book misses the promise.**

## CS-4 — DATADOG FY2026-Q2 · THE GAP

**Header:** THE CALL IS AHEAD OF THE PRINT

**Body:** Datadog ran a confident call on a slightly negative print z.
Demand: level +1.9, surprise +1.2, `quant_z` −0.29, gap 1.49. They sold
36% growth, record sequential adds, non-AI high-20s. Surprise says they
beat the Street’s tape. The z says that beat is ordinary versus their

de-risked the largest customer. Confidence is still +1.6. Novelty −0.8

Bits SRE now GA (was private beta). 30% of revenue in R&D. 25%+ margin
goal (printed 23% / 25% FCF). India/Brazil headcount due this quarter.

**Footer:** Trade the slope, not “raised guide.” **Words and print agree on the deceleration. That is the position.**

## CS-6 — WHAT IT MEANS

**Header:** ROZ DOES NOT STAMP “BULLISH”

**Body:**

- **Divergence** (agree False + a large gap) is the use case. The call
  is saying something the print z does not. DDOG demand. LITE margins.
- **Slope vs adjective.** Level can stay high while delta goes negative.
  DDOG guidance. Trade the slope.
- **Novelty is an object detector**, not louder tone. DDOG’s
  largest-customer caveat. LITE’s first ELS PO / 70–80% pump share.
  New objects become trees.
- **Same-direction agreement** is confirmation, not a secret. LITE
  demand and FCF.
- **A tree remembers what a quarter rank forgets.** LITE’s $600M clock
  is already behind them. DDOG’s FedRAMP clock is still open.
- **On XLK (not these names)** raw tone reversed. Narrative–quant
  agreement was flat three weeks, ~0.10 by weeks 6–9, ~0.17 by ~day 90.
  These Independents show the machinery. They are not a live IC.
