## What to say

1. **Do not treat 0_56 as “the” horizon.** The production demand primary
   is not a T+7 print. core22 demand `quant_z_pit`: 0_14 -0.007152,
   14_35 0.045755, 35_56 0.014354 (`expe-horizon-anatomy-v1`, 40
   periods). It peaks mid-window.

2. **Guidance surprise is front-loaded.** 0_14 0.050003 > 35_56 0.035452
   (`expe-horizon-anatomy-v1`). This is the one Line-1 print that held.

3. **Software novelty is not a T+7 pop.** 0_14 0.126509 vs level
   -0.056907; 35_56 0.070562 vs -0.079985. Late-bucket jackknife min
   0.025389 (IBM out), 7/7 beat level (`expe-horizon-anatomy-v1`,
   `expe-horizon-anatomy-v2`). Mid-window hole: 14_35 novelty 0.026608
   loses to level 0.106195.

4. **Late designer confidence is back-loaded.** Late 20 periods, 35_56
   0.191130 vs 0_14 -0.098494 (`expe-horizon-anatomy-v1`). Every name
   drop stays positive (min 0.129893 AMD). The level-over-change beat
   in that bucket dies when AMD is out (0.129893 vs change 0.207672,
   `expe-horizon-anatomy-v2`).

## What not to say

- “Prints are front-loaded” as a book-wide fact. Demand quant is not.
- Late designer confidence “beats change in the late bucket after
  every name drop.” Sign yes; uniqueness vs change no.
- Anything about holdouts or promotion.
